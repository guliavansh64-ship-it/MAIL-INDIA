Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Entity: MAIL INDIA Cloud Infrastructure · Jurisdiction: Republic of India

1. Purpose and Philosophy

MAIL INDIA is dedicated to maintaining the highest domain reputation, IP pool cleanliness, and primary deliverability for Indian software businesses and developers. This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines mandatory sending standards across our REST APIs and SMTP relays.

2. Permitted Email Communications

MAIL INDIA is designed specifically for:

  • Transactional Dispatches: One-Time Passwords (OTPs), authentication links, multi-factor verification codes.
  • Account Lifecycle Alerts: Password resets, security login notices, profile change verifications.
  • Operational Notifications: Invoices, payment receipts, order shipment updates, billing statements.
  • Opted-In Relationship Emails: Product updates, customer support replies, and verified double opt-in newsletters.

3. Strictly Prohibited Sending Practices

Zero Tolerance for Unsolicited Mail: You may never use MAIL INDIA to send unsolicited emails (spam). Purchased, rented, scraped, harvested, or third-party mailing lists are strictly prohibited.

The following activities result in immediate account termination and permanent blacklisting:

  • Sending to email addresses obtained through scraping, crawling, or directory harvesting.
  • Phishing, spear-phishing, social engineering, credential harvesting, or deceptive impersonation of banking or government institutions.
  • Distributing malware, viruses, ransomware, malicious attachment links, or illicit material.
  • Falsifying or obfuscating email headers, origin routing IPs, or Reply-To addresses.
  • Operating pyramid schemes, deceptive crypto promotions, get-rich-quick scams, or counterfeit goods operations.

4. Deliverability Thresholds & Automated Bounce Shields

To preserve our domestic IP reputation, your sending volume must maintain:

  • Hard Bounce Rate: Must remain strictly below 5.0%. Accounts exceeding this threshold trigger automated rate limiting.
  • Spam Complaint Rate: Must remain strictly below 0.1% (less than 1 complaint per 1,000 sent emails).
  • Suppression Enforcement: Our platform automatically adds any hard bounce or spam complaint to your account's permanent suppression list to prevent repeated failed dispatches and preserve your credits.

5. Mandatory Unsubscribe & Identity Transparency

Non-transactional communications must clearly identify the sender, include a valid physical address, and feature a functional, 1-click unsubscribe mechanism that honors recipient opt-outs within 48 hours.

6. Violations & Abuse Reporting

If you detect any spam, abuse, or unauthorized transmission originating from MAIL INDIA infrastructure, please report it immediately to our 24/7 security team:

Email: abuse@mailindia.in
Our security team investigates every report within 2 hours and takes immediate remedial action.
