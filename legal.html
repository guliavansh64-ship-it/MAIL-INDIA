Key Sovereign Cloud Commitments

DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Operates strictly within India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act framework. Data principals can request rectification or complete erasure with statutory verification.

Sovereign Indian Storage

Databases, authentication credentials, and cryptographic vaults are physically located in Mumbai and Bengaluru edge clusters. Zero unconsented overseas replication.

Zero Body Retention

We do not inspect, monetize, or permanently store outgoing email HTML or text bodies. Payloads are purged from volatile memory immediately after provider delivery acceptance.

2048-bit DKIM & TLS 1.3

Outbound dispatches are cryptographically sealed with 2048-bit RSA keys and transmitted over TLS 1.3 with high-entropy cryptographic ciphers.

Statutory Scope & Legal Entity

MAIL INDIA Cloud Infrastructure is a transactional email and messaging platform providing REST APIs, SMTP relay nodes, and Google Workspace OAuth dispatch mechanisms to registered developers and businesses throughout India.

Indian Legal Framework: This platform is governed by the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and rules framed thereunder.

By accessing the API or creating an account, users consent to data processing for dispatch telemetry, fraud prevention, and wallet accounting as detailed in the sub-policies accessible below.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Jurisdiction: Republic of India
Open Full Page

1. Digital Personal Data Protection Act Compliance

MAIL INDIA collects tenant profile details, authenticated Google identities, and transmission metadata strictly to fulfill email dispatch requests. Message bodies are processed in volatile memory and are not permanently archived.

2. Sovereign Storage & Key Security

All customer records and secret keys are stored in sovereign Indian regions. Plaintext API keys (format mb_live_...) are hashed using SHA-256 before database storage.

3. Log Lifecycles & Self-Service Erasure

Transmission logs are automatically purged after 30 calendar days. Users may initiate full account anonymization or export telemetry at any time.

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: November 2026 · Master Service Agreement
Open Full Page

1. Service Scope & Availability

MAIL INDIA provides high-deliverability transactional cloud email infrastructure with a target service availability of 99.9%. Rate limits apply per tenant tier to safeguard cluster stability.

2. Credit Wallet & Instant UPI Top-ups

Standard dispatches consume 1 credit per accepted email (₹1 = 5 Credits, meaning ₹0.20 per dispatch). Google Workspace Relays operate at ₹0.10 per dispatch (10 credits / ₹1). Credits are reserved prior to transmission and permanently deducted only upon upstream provider acceptance.

3. Refunds & Ledger Reconciliations

Emails rejected prior to transmission (e.g., suppressed or blacklisted recipients) immediately restore reserved credits to the user wallet.

Acceptable Use Policy

Anti-Spam Standards & Deliverability Requirements
Open Full Page

1. Transactional Integrity & Anti-Spam Requirements

MAIL INDIA strictly prohibits spam, unsolicited bulk marketing, phishing, and list harvesting. Tenants must maintain an explicit opt-in relationship with all recipients.

2. Deliverability Thresholds

Reputation Ceilings: Accounts with hard bounce rates exceeding 5% or spam complaint rates exceeding 0.1% are automatically throttled or suspended.

3. Automated Suppression & Safeguards

Recipients that report spam, unsubscribe, or generate hard bounces are immediately committed to the tenant suppression list to protect domain deliverability.
