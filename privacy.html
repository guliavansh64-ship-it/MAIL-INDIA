Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Entity: MAIL INDIA Cloud Infrastructure · Jurisdiction: Republic of India

1. Introduction & Statutory Framework

MAIL INDIA ("we," "our," or "the Platform") provides developer-oriented transactional email infrastructure, cloud SMTP relays, and messaging APIs. We operate in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data and transmission metadata are collected, encrypted, processed, and safeguarded when you access our services, websites, APIs, and client dashboards.

2. Indian Data Residency & Sovereign Storage

Domestic Cloud Architecture: All production databases, authentication stores, cryptographic key vaults, and message queues are hosted in sovereign Indian data center regions (Mumbai and Bengaluru edge nodes).

We do not transfer your tenant credentials, secret API keys, recipient logs, or customer data to overseas jurisdictions without explicit authorization or regulatory requirement.

3. Information We Collect

A. Account and Profile Data

When you register for a MAIL INDIA account, we collect your name, authenticated email address, Google profile identifier (if connecting via Google OAuth), company name, and voluntary contact numbers.

B. API Credentials & Authentication Secrets

API keys generated within the dashboard are cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 before disk persistence. The plaintext API key is presented to you only once upon generation and cannot be viewed by our staff.

C. Transmission Metadata & Zero-Retention Policy on Message Bodies

To provide delivery telemetry, we record transmission logs consisting of: recipient address, sender address, subject line, message ID, transmission timestamp, relay route (Google API vs Cloud SMTP), latency in milliseconds, and provider return status codes.

Message Body Redaction: We do not monetize, analyze, or permanently retain outgoing email HTML or plain-text bodies. Message payload bytes are held in volatile in-memory buffers strictly for the duration required to achieve upstream provider acceptance, after which they are promptly flushed.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate API requests and verify cryptographic token signatures.
  • Relay transactional emails to upstream mailbox providers (Google, Yahoo, Microsoft).
  • Maintain the immutable wallet ledger and account for credit reservations (₹1 = 5 Credits).
  • Prevent spam, fraud, credential compromise, and dictionary recipient attacks.
  • Provide searchable transmission logs and webhook delivery notifications in your dashboard.

5. Log Retention & Self-Service Data Erasure

Transmission logs and audit events are retained for 30 calendar days for operational verification, after which they are automatically purged. Users may export all personal account data or trigger complete GDPR/DPDP deletion at any time via Account & Security > Export My Data or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

6. Security Controls & Encryption

All communication with MAIL INDIA is protected by TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Sensitive third-party tokens and Google OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 with authenticated envelope keys. Outgoing emails are signed using 2048-bit RSA DKIM cryptographic keys.
