Frequently Asked Questions

Do purchased email credits expire? ▾
No. Credits purchased on Mail India have lifetime validity and will remain available in your wallet until consumed by provider-accepted emails.
What happens if an email fails to deliver or bounces? ▾
You are only charged for emails accepted by upstream SMTP providers. If an email fails pre-send checks, suppression, or provider rejection, the reserved credit is immediately released and refunded back to your wallet.
Can I claim GST Input Tax Credit (ITC)? ▾
Yes! Enter your company GSTIN in the Invoices & Receipts page. Every UPI recharge generates a GST-compliant tax invoice (SAC 9984) with 18% GST itemized for Input Tax Credit claims.
Which UPI payment apps are supported? ▾
All standard Indian UPI apps are supported including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, Navi, Amazon Pay, and all netbanking UPI handles.
